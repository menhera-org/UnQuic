Plain UDP and TCP between the two hosts of the real path, November 26. No daemon:
examples/udp_load sends from one host's outer address to the other's, sixteen flows on
distinct port pairs, 1452-byte payloads, 10 s per line, two sending and two receiving
processes unless a line says otherwise. Rates are payload Gbit/s. No receive socket
overflowed except where noted. TCP is iperf3 3.20 with default settings.
