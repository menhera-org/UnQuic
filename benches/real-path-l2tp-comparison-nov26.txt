A static kernel L2TPv3 Ethernet pseudowire against the daemon on the real path, November 26.
benches/pair_side.sh, sixteen QinQ flows of 1522-byte frames, both directions loaded alike,
10 s points, one round. Rates are the load tool's own counts in Gbit/s of 1472-byte
payloads; cpu-s is CPU seconds of the load processes over the 10 s (snd, rcv).

  l2ip, l2udp  KERNEL_TUNNEL=l2tp with L2TP_ENCAP=ip and udp: no daemon. The tunnel device
               takes no segmentation offload, so the load tool sends frames singly; four
               sending and four receiving processes a host. Each 1570-byte tunnel packet
               leaves as two IPv6 fragments. The sending processes' CPU includes the
               tunnel's transmit work; its receive work is kernel softirq on the hosts'
               machines and is not in any number here.
  uq1          the daemon (836057a, four workers), the same single frames; two sending
               and two receiving processes a host.
  uq44         the daemon, 44-segment GSO trains, as in the entries above.
